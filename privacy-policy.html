IntelligentEra (“IntelligentEra,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a technology company that develops software, websites, mobile applications, SaaS products, AI agents, AI-integrated software, machine learning systems, and related digital and technical solutions.

1. About IntelligentEra

IntelligentEra provides technology and software development services, including website development, mobile and web application development, SaaS development and operation, AI agent development, AI-powered automation, AI-integrated software, machine learning systems, software architecture and infrastructure design, digital transformation solutions, technical consulting, and continuous software improvement.

IntelligentEra also develops and operates its own SaaS products, which may process information provided by users or connected business systems.

4. Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Some IntelligentEra products use artificial intelligence, machine learning, automated processing, or AI agents. Depending on the product, information submitted by users may be processed by AI systems to provide requested functionality.

AI Training

Unless explicitly stated otherwise for a particular product or agreed upon with a customer, IntelligentEra does not automatically assume that confidential customer business data may be used to train a general-purpose AI model.

Where we intend to use customer data for model training, product improvement, research, or another materially different purpose, we will do so in accordance with the applicable agreement, disclosed terms, or applicable law.

AI Agents and Automated Systems

IntelligentEra may develop AI agents and automation systems that interact with external communication platforms and services, including systems for WhatsApp communication, Instagram communication, customer support, automated responses, business workflows, and data analysis.

AI-generated responses may not always be accurate, complete, appropriate, or suitable for a particular purpose. Users and customers should review AI-generated information where appropriate.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information as a product. We may disclose information to third parties when reasonably necessary to provide, operate, secure, or improve our services.

Service Providers

These may include cloud hosting providers, database and software infrastructure providers, analytics providers, authentication services, email delivery providers, payment processors, security providers, customer support tools, AI or machine-learning infrastructure, and monitoring or logging services.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful governmental requests, court orders or legal processes, protect the rights, property, or safety of IntelligentEra or others, or investigate fraud, security issues, or illegal activity.

Business Transactions

If IntelligentEra is involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, sale of assets, or similar transaction, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

Depending on the service and circumstances, these measures may include:

  • Access controls
  • Authentication mechanisms
  • Encryption where appropriate
  • Secure infrastructure
  • Monitoring and logging
  • Security testing
  • Backups
  • Least-privilege access practices
  • Administrative and technical safeguards

No internet transmission, software system, cloud infrastructure, or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain accounts, fulfill contractual obligations, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, detect and prevent abuse or security incidents, comply with legal obligations, and protect our legitimate business interests.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information, service involved, contractual requirements, and applicable law.
